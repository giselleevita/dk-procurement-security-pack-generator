# Three-minute recruiter demonstration

1. Select **Try synthetic demo**. The free service may need about a minute to wake.
2. On the dashboard, explain that the 12 results are synthetic and disposable.
3. Select **Run verified demo**.
4. Show the four stages: collect, sign, independently verify, and tamper-test.
5. Expand **Artifact fingerprints** and explain that every pack payload is bound to the signed manifest.
6. Download the ZIP only if the reviewer wants to inspect the portable evidence.

The strongest engineering discussion is the boundary: verification rejects incomplete manifests,
unexpected or duplicate files, unsafe paths, oversized archives, and any changed payload.

Never upload real customer evidence to the public free demo.
